Frequently asked questions

How do I book an event?

Message us on WhatsApp or call with your event date, type and venue. We will discuss the setup and share a quotation.

Can I book just one service?

Yes. You can choose a single service such as cakes, hampers, photography or lights and sound, or let us coordinate the complete celebration.

What are your working hours?

We are available daily from 10 AM to 10 PM at Pazhayangadi, Kannur.

Do you also arrange tours and travel?

Yes, through Dream Love Holidays. Contact us for tour packages, family trips and group tours.
